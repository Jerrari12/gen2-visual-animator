## What v2609 changed (measured; the vault has the numbers with their provenance)

1. **A tear-away front on every size.** A 1.9 mm panel closing the front opening, back face 4.30 mm behind the front
   plane, front face 2.40 mm behind it at a 3.5 mm rim and 3.10 mm over the centre. The same panel the Gridfinity v2609
   drawers carry (the same two areas on 185-1W-1H). Joey: optional; a faceplate slides on with it in; a faceplate WITH
   its back cover collides, so remove one or the other.
2. **A plug in each side-wall window** (2.0 x 3.0 x 4.4 mm, a separate closed body, 0.8 mm proud of the 1.2 mm wall).
   The window itself was already there in May. Purpose unknown - ask Joey.
3. **The 2H print-support posts are gone** (the May 2H STLs had two, 3 mm in front of the face).
4. **A bed flange on eleven sizes** (every 3H, and 240-2W-15H): 1.2 mm tall round the back bottom corners, 3.4 mm
   beyond the sides and 2.8 mm beyond the back. The July GLBs did not have it (the May 3H STLs did). The builder cuts
   it off; the customer's print has it.
5. **185's front-recess blocks** (6 x 2.4 x 4 mm pairs at the cell boundaries on 185 2W-4W, most heights): already in
   May and in the July GLBs; kept. Purpose unknown.
6. **Two copies of the release:** the zip of 2026-09-30 and the folder, 25 files re-saved 2026-10-05 (a back-wall
   feature 4 mm shorter). Built from the folder. Which copy the stores have is not established - ask Joey.

## How the GLBs were made, and what was verified

- `blender --background --factory-startup --python build_decor_drawer_blend.py -- "<blend>" "D:\Claude - Model Sharing\GEN2\v2609 Decor Drawers"`
  (Blender 5.1.1). Identity import; `remove_doubles` 1e-4; on the eleven flanged sizes, after checking that the
  oversize is the known flange (in the bottom 1.5 mm, at most 4 mm out) and that the mesh has no boundary edge, three
  plane cuts ONE MICRON outside the standard envelope, each capped with the loops lying in that plane and only those
  (a cut exactly on the wall plane deletes the wall's own faces, measured on 115-1W-3H), then the four 1-micron slivers
  the cut leaves in the micron band dropped and nothing else. Fails closed on: span (88W-13 x L-5.7 x 22/50/78/106/162
  within 0.05), closed shells (every edge on an even number of faces - the plugs touch the walls, so four is
  legitimate), floor down (downward area in the bottom plane at least half the footprint, upward area in the top plane
  at most a fifth), the panel at the low-y end (front-facing area 2.3 to 3.2 mm behind the front at least 0.3 x the
  opening and 1.2 x the matching band at the back; measured 1.34 to 9.4 as authored, at most 0.75 if a drawer were
  turned round), and on 1H and 1.5H the right-hand side on +x (a 56 mm2 recess in the right wall's outer face, none on
  the left). An x mirror is NOT detectable on 05H (mirror-symmetric to the vertex), 2H or 3H (near-equal features on
  both walls); for this run the July-GLB comparison below rules it out. `build-report.log` lists every drawer with its
  span, cap areas (3.68 / 3.68 / 10.08 mm2 on every cut drawer, 4 slivers each) and the areas the checks used.
- `python gen2_batch.py "<run-jobs.json>"`: six jobs, `depth_mode` center, no materials (the July GLBs had none),
  meshopt via glTF-Transform 4.5.1. ⚠ Delete the 94 `.lib.glb` first: the batch compresses only where no lib exists,
  so a rerun over the old files re-exports the raws and leaves the served files stale (the README says so; the July
  files are backed up as above). The batch re-verifies every raw in each folder and reports the 27 side covers as
  `no_materials` failures (they carry BODY/FACE zones by design; their own job opts in); all 94 Decor rows pass.
- `compare_glbs.py` old vs new raw GLBs: envelope within 0.001 mm on all 94 (0.000 on 83); the July vertices recur in
  the new within 0.05 mm on 100 percent of 69 sizes and 99.5-99.6 percent of the 25 re-saved sizes; the front (the
  panel) at +Z on all 94 with the back band unchanged; shells = July's + the two plugs on all 94. (The Gridfinity run
  of 2026-09-18 used the same frame argument: identity import lands the STLs' low-y end at glTF +Z.)
- gen2-parts: the 94 lib files copied and md5-checked (0 mismatches), one commit on the branch; the batch was run a
  final time from the committed script version and the files compared again by md5 (see the vault section).
- Planner, in the check worktree with `GEN2_PARTS_LOCAL=1`: `node --test` 561 tests, 556 pass, 0 fail, 5 skipped
  (parts-exist walks every generated manifest against the new files); `node --test e2e/workspaces.mjs` 60 of 60 pass
  in 238 s on the GPU. Nothing in the planner changed. The goldens were not re-blessed: every kit's drawers now have a
  closed front, so the golden images would change by design, not by a fault.
